# Disclaimer

This project is research and education software. It is **not investment advice** and the authors are not SEBI-registered advisers.

- v1 is **paper trading only**. It cannot place live orders.
- AI-generated arguments and verdicts can be wrong. The scoreboard shows how past verdicts did; past results do not predict future results.
- Most retail derivatives traders lose money (SEBI's FY26 study: 87.7% of individual F&O traders lost money). Do not risk money you cannot afford to lose.
- Retail algorithmic trading in India is regulated by SEBI and exchanges. Check current rules before connecting any broker.
- Simulated data is the default. It is random and only for demos.
